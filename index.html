Step 1
Upload drawings and prepare the project

Start by uploading your PDF plan set. The app renders every page so you can review each sheet, pan and zoom, and identify symbols for takeoff.

What to do first
Upload your PDF drawings, check page thumbnails, and confirm the project location. Verify the plan quality before you begin takeoff.
Quick tip
Use the built-in page navigator to move between sheets and confirm every drawing is included.
Step 2
Count symbols across every page

Draw a selection box around a fixture, outlet, or device. Real Cost finds every matching assembly and lets you verify counts instantly.

What you’ll learn
How to place selection boxes, compare matches, adjust counts, and save symbol groups for estimating.
Quick tip
Review the sample symbol carefully when you count similar fixtures or devices.
Step 3
Build your bid page and review pricing

Use the bid page to configure materials, labour, overhead and markup. The tutorial shows how totals update and how to compare pricing across cities.

What to expect
See how material and labour are calculated, and how overhead and markup affect the final estimate.
Quick tip
Use the estimate graph to confirm the value breakdown before generating the quote.
Step 4
Generate your quote letter

Finalize your bid and create a branded quote letter. The tutorial explains export options and client-ready formatting.

What you’ll do
Generate a professional PDF quote letter with scope, totals, and project details ready to send.
Quick tip
Review totals and scope before export so the letter is client-ready on the first try.

Getting started
What is Real Cost and who is it for?
+
Real Cost is a digital estimation platform for trade contractors. It lets your team upload PDF drawings, count symbols, build a full bid, and generate a professional quote letter — all without spreadsheets or outsourcing. It's designed for electrical, mechanical, plumbing, fire alarm, and other trade contractors across Canada.
How do I start using Real Cost?
+
Open the app at the link on this page. Start your 14-day free trial — no credit card required. Upload a PDF drawing set, create a project, and your team can begin estimating immediately.
What file types can I upload?
+
You can upload multi-page PDF drawing sets. The platform renders each page on a navigable digital takeoff canvas. Most standard architectural and engineering drawing exports work directly.
Which trades are supported?
+
Real Cost supports electrical, mechanical/HVAC, plumbing, fire alarm, voice & data, security systems, audio/visual, heat tracing, and mechanical control. More trades are added regularly.
Features & platform
How does symbol auto-count work?
+
Draw a rectangle around any reference symbol on a drawing — a light fixture, outlet, smoke detector, etc. The platform uses pattern matching to find and count all identical symbols across every page of the project drawings, instantly.
What is the Bid Page?
+
The Bid Page is where your estimate comes together. Once takeoff is done, it auto-calculates material costs, labour hours, overhead percentage, markup, and project duration for different crew sizes. All figures update live as you make changes.
What is Canadian city-based pricing?
+
Material rates in Real Cost automatically adjust based on your city's regional pricing tier. L1, L2, and L3 tiers cover cities including Quebec, Ottawa, Toronto, Montreal, Calgary, and Vancouver. This ensures your estimates reflect local market costs without manual adjustments.
How does the quote letter generator work?
+
Once your bid is complete, one click generates a professional branded PDF or Word quote letter ready to send to your client. It includes all project details, scope summary, and your total bid price in a clean, professional format.
Can my whole team use it?
+
Yes. Real Cost supports team accounts with owner and estimator roles. As the owner you can assign projects to sub-estimators, track their progress, and manage the team subscription from one dashboard. Each user is billed at $10/month or $80/year.
